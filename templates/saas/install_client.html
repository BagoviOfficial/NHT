What Happens During Installation?

When you install a client, the platform automatically:

  • Allocates a dedicated tenant space with strict data isolation.
  • Assigns a unique cryptographic tracking prefix (e.g. TWK-XXXXXXXX).
  • Creates the client's initial Super Administrator account.
  • Initializes customized 80mm thermal receipt branding and landscape A4 PDF manifests.
  • Pre-populates default packaging categories (Box, Sack, Carton, Envelope, Crate) and starter transit routes.
